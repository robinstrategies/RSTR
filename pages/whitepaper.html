Whitepaper

Robin Strategy is a short-term investment DAO built on Robinhood Chain. Its purpose is simple: to strategically deploy into Ethereum, the world’s greatest asset, and into related memes, stocks, and commodities offered across Robinhood Chain.

A meme-powered treasury experiment with a community, a strategy, and a clear directional bias toward Ethereum or similar beta-based derivatives.

Ethereum is the core vehicle. Around it sits a broader opportunity set made up of adjacent internet culture, tokenized market exposure, and selected onchain instruments that fit the Robin Strategy worldview.

Season 1 (prelude): create the coin and the community to perpetuate Ethereum’s success.

Season 2 (growth): deploy raised treasury to take advantage of short-term opportunities.

Season 3 (climax): gather assets to become an Ethereum node or similar sustaining role.

Season 4: TBA.

Launch details: Raisehood on Robinhood Chain soon.
