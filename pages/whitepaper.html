RobinStrategy is a parody DAO built for people who are tired of financial theater pretending to be destiny. It uses humor, strong branding, community storytelling, and treasury coordination to create a meme-native organization with a social identity and a strategic capital allocation narrative.

The project aims to combine a recognizable meme-finance brand, a community treasury, collective decision-making, recurring visual storytelling, and entertaining communications that still convey strategy.
