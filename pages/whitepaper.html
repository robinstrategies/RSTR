Whitepaper

Short. Sharp. Green.

Robin Strategies is a short-term investment vehicle DAO built on Robinhood Chain. Its purpose is simple: to strategically deploy into Ethereum, the world’s greatest asset, and into related memes, stocks, and commodities offered across Robinhood Chain.

This is not a bloated institution pretending to be sacred. It is a meme-powered treasury experiment with a community, a strategy, and a clear directional bias toward Ethereum.

Ethereum is the core vehicle. Around it sits a broader opportunity set made up of adjacent internet culture, tokenized market exposure, and selected onchain instruments that fit the Robin Strategies worldview.

Season 1 (the pilot) is simple: create the coin and the community to perpetuate Ethereum’s success.

Robin Strategies is designed to be public-facing, community-driven, and easy to follow. The treasury thesis is meant to be visible. The memes are part of the delivery system. The strategy is the point.
