What This Is

RobinStrategy is a satirical internet-native organization exploring how DAO governance, treasury strategy, and visual storytelling can become one shared meme-finance identity.

What This Is Not

It is not a copy of a public company, not a real-world executive fan club, and not a promise of returns. It is parody with structure, style, and social coordination.
